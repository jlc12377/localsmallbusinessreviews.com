About New York Coverage

A metro list, not a city limit.

These pages follow the New York metro (five boroughs, Westchester, Long Island, northern NJ) research set. The towns below are the ones recorded in that file, after obvious spelling duplicates were combined.

Vernon, New York, New York (Manhattan), Newark, North Arlington, North Bergen, Nutley, Orange, Paramus, Pearl River, Pleasantville, Port Washington, Queens, Queens (Kew Gardens), Ramsey, Richmond Hill, Ridgefield Park, Ridgewood, Rockville Centre, Roslyn, Rutherford, Rye Brook, Scarsdale, Scotch Plains, Short Hills, Sleepy Hollow, Somers, Springfield, Staten Island, Summit, Sunnyside, Union, Uniondale, Verona, Wayne, Weehawken, West Orange, Westfield, White Plains, Whitestone, Woodside, Wyckoff, Yonkers, Yorktown Heights.
